## Research sources and verified facts

- Services Australia, [PBS Safety Net thresholds](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/pbs-safety-net-thresholds?context=22016), last updated 1 January 2026. 2026 thresholds are $1,748.20 general and $277.20 concession. The calendar-year tally normally resets on 1 January. Before reaching the threshold, qualifying patient contributions are up to $25 general or $7.70 concession. After reaching the threshold and having the card issued, the patient contribution is up to $7.70 general or free for concession card holders. An additional premium for some expensive brands may still apply.
- Services Australia, [How to reach the threshold sooner as a family](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/how-to-reach-threshold-sooner-family?context=22016), last updated 14 September 2026. Qualifying PBS amounts may be combined for an eligible family. Categories include married couples who are not separated, de facto couples with or without dependent children, and a single person with dependent children. Dependent child guidance includes a child under 16, or a full-time student under 25 attending school, college or university and substantially financially supported. Do not infer eligibility in ambiguous cases; ask a pharmacist.
- Services Australia, [Keep track of your PBS medicines](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/keep-track-your-pbs-medicines?context=22016), last updated 14 September 2026. Request an expenditure printout from every pharmacy used. A regular pharmacy may maintain a computer record. Do not imply automatic cross-pharmacy aggregation.
- Services Australia [PB240 form PDF](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/sites/default/files/pb240-2008en-f.pdf). The currently linked PDF is three pages. It says questions 1–6 must be typed before printing; complete the form on a computer, print and sign it, and give it to the pharmacist each time a PBS/RPBS medicine is supplied. It does not explicitly instruct users to print all three pages. Our practical recommendation is to print the complete linked three-page PDF so its medicine-record page is available. The generic [PB240 landing page](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/pb240), last updated 17 November 2022, describes a two-page form and says to hand pages 1 and 2 to the pharmacist. Explain this discrepancy without treating the printed 25 November 2026 date as a policy update. The pharmacist records qualifying amounts and can help apply for a Safety Net card after the threshold.
- Services Australia, [PBS Safety Net card application](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/pbs-safety-net-card-application?context=22016), last updated 14 September 2026. Apply when the threshold is reached using completed records with help from a pharmacist. A card is valid from issue through the calendar year. A refund for a qualifying overpayment may be available; it is not guaranteed.
- Services Australia, [Medicines that count towards the threshold](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/medicines-count-towards-threshold?context=22016), last updated 14 September 2026. Amount paid does not always equal amount counted. Higher brand premiums and early supplies may be excluded; some non-PBS medicines supplied to public-hospital outpatients may count. Ask the pharmacist which amount qualifies.

The threshold page was last updated 1 January 2026. The family, records, card-application and medicines-count pages were last updated 14 September 2026. The PB240 landing page was last updated 17 November 2022. Dates printed on copies or retrieval artefacts around 25–26 November are not policy-change dates.

## Blog Content

### Summary (writer reference, not a mandatory public box)
PBS Safety Net is support for PBS prescription medicine costs, not the Medicare Safety Net. For 2026, the thresholds are $1,748.20 general and $277.20 concession, but not every dollar paid necessarily counts. Keep records from every pharmacy, ask a pharmacist whether family grouping applies, and use the complete current PB240 PDF. Premiums may remain, and family eligibility or refunds are not automatic.

### Sections

#### PBS Safety Net은 Medicare Safety Net과 다른 약값 제도예요
It tracks qualifying patient contributions for PBS medicines during the calendar year. The 2026 general threshold is $1,748.20 and the concession threshold is $277.20. The tally normally resets on 1 January. These are 2026 figures; do not present them as timeless. Source: Services Australia, PBS Safety Net thresholds, updated 1 January 2026.

#### 여러 약국을 이용했다면 각 약국 기록을 모아요
Ask each pharmacy used for a PBS expenditure printout for the calendar year. A regular pharmacy may keep a computer record, but do not promise that records from different pharmacies combine automatically. Services Australia says to request printouts from every pharmacy. Source: Services Australia, Keep track of your PBS medicines, updated 14 September 2026.

#### 가족 합산은 가족 유형과 조건을 확인해요
Eligible groupings can include married couples who are not separated, de facto couples with or without dependent children, and a single person with dependent children. The two dependent-child categories are: a dependent child under 16; or a dependent full-time student under 25 who attends school, college or university and receives substantial financial support. PB240 uses the relationship codes DC (dependent child under 16) and DS (dependent full-time student under 25). Avoid deciding ambiguous family cases from a short checklist; ask a pharmacist. Sources: Services Australia family guide and PB240 PDF.

#### PB240는 웹 안내와 연결 PDF의 쪽수가 달라요
The generic PB240 landing page (last updated 17 November 2022) describes a two-page form, while the currently linked PDF has three pages. The PDF says questions 1–6 must be typed before printing, then says to print and sign the completed form. It does not explicitly say to print every page. As a practical step, print the complete linked three-page PDF so its medicine-record page is available, then give the form to the pharmacist each time a PBS/RPBS medicine is supplied. Ask the pharmacist if a field or family category is unclear. The pharmacist records qualifying amounts and can help with the card application after the threshold.

#### 지불액과 Safety Net에 잡히는 금액은 다를 수 있어요
For eligible PBS medicines, the 2026 patient contribution before the threshold is up to $25 general or $7.70 concession. After reaching the threshold, an issued Safety Net card must be presented to receive the applicable patient contribution: up to $7.70 general or free for concession card holders. A higher brand premium may remain. Early supplies may be excluded, and some public-hospital outpatient non-PBS medicines may count. Ask the pharmacist what portion of a particular supply qualifies. Sources: Services Australia thresholds page, updated 1 January 2026, and medicines-count page, updated 14 September 2026.

#### 카드와 환급은 약사와 기록을 확인한 뒤 진행해요
Once the threshold is reached, take completed records to the pharmacist, who can help with the card application. The card runs from issue through that calendar year. A qualifying refund for overpayment may be available, but it is not guaranteed. Sources: Services Australia card application, updated 14 September 2026; thresholds, updated 1 January 2026.

### FAQ questions (research reference; publish only questions not already answered)
- **Q: 가족 PBS Safety Net은 자동으로 합산되나요?**
  자동 합산으로 단정하지 말고 가족 유형과 약국 기록을 약사에게 확인해요. 각 약국의 연간 지출 내역도 모아요.
- **Q: PBS Safety Net 카드가 있으면 약이 전부 무료인가요?**
  아니에요. 2026년 일반 카드 본인부담은 약당 최대 $7.70, concession은 무료지만 일부 고가 브랜드 추가 부담금은 남을 수 있어요.
- **Q: PBS 약값으로 낸 돈은 전부 기준액에 포함되나요?**
  항상 그렇지는 않아요. 브랜드 추가 부담금이나 너무 이른 조제는 제외될 수 있고, 일부 공립병원 외래 비-PBS 약은 포함될 수 있어 약사에게 확인해요.
