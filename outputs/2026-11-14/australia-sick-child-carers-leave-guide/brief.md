## Blog Content

### Summary (writer reference, not a mandatory public box)
An employee in the national Fair Work system may use sick/carer's leave to care for an ill child who is immediate family. Paid leave shares one balance; unpaid leave depends on employment type and remaining balance. Notify the employer promptly, state the expected period, and check evidence requirements. NSW state public-sector/local-government workers may be covered by another system.

### Sections

#### 아이가 아플 때 어떤 휴가를 쓸 수 있나요?
Children can be immediate family for carer's leave. It concerns care/support because of illness, injury, or an unexpected emergency, not ordinary childcare arrangements. These Fair Work rules apply to national-system employees; NSW state public-sector and local-government staff are outside that system. Sources A, E.

#### 유급 돌봄휴가는 내 병가와 잔액을 함께 써요
Paid sick leave and carer's leave use the same balance. Full-time employees receive 10 days per year; part-time entitlement is proportional to ordinary hours. It accrues progressively from the first day and unused balance carries over. The 10 days are combined, not separate pools. Source B.

#### 캐주얼이거나 유급 잔액이 없으면 어떻게 하나요?
Casual employees may use two days unpaid carer's leave for each qualifying occasion. Full-time/part-time employees qualify when no paid sick/carer's leave is available. This is not two days per child or a yearly allowance. Source C.

#### 회사에는 언제 무엇을 알려야 하나요?
Tell the employer as soon as possible and give the expected period away from work. The notice may be after the leave begins. Suggested wording is an editorial example, not a prescribed legal form. Source D.

#### 하루만 쉬어도 증빙을 준비해야 하나요?
Evidence may be requested even for one day or less. A medical certificate or statutory declaration can be examples, but do not promise a particular document will be accepted. Ask what is reasonable for the situation and check the applicable award or agreement. End by checking the employee's balance and workplace evidence process. Source D.

### FAQ questions (research reference; do not repeat as public FAQ)
- **Q: 아이가 아프면 carer's leave를 쓸 수 있나요?**
  National-system employees may use it to provide care/support for an ill immediate-family member, including a child; ordinary childcare needs are different.
- **Q: 아이 돌봄휴가 증빙은 며칠부터 필요한가요?**
  Evidence may be requested for one day or less; check workplace instruments and what reasonably establishes the entitlement.
- **Q: 캐주얼도 아이가 아플 때 돌봄휴가를 쓸 수 있나요?**
  A casual may take two unpaid days per qualifying occasion.
